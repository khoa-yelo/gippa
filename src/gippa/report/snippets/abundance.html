Gene abundance is the relative molar amount of each gene's protein in this sample, known only up to one constant per run: ratios and ranks between genes carry meaning, absolute values do not, and runs cannot be compared without normalisation. With peptide intensities, the default is iBAQ, the summed intensity of a gene's quantified peptides divided by its number of theoretically observable peptides (Top3, the mean of the three largest intensities, is the alternative). Otherwise a supplied protein table is used. Values are log2. A detected gene without a quantified value is filled with the run median by default; a gene that is never detected is never filled.
