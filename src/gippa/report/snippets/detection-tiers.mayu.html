The peptides carry q-values or posterior error probabilities (PEPs), but no decoys pair with genes well enough for the picked estimate. At each threshold, the expected number of false peptides is the sum of their PEPs, or the threshold times the peptide count when PEPs are missing. False peptides are assumed to land on genes in proportion to each gene's observable peptides (MAYU); the estimate counts how many detected genes owe their detection to false peptides alone. That share is the gene FDR, and a gene is detected when its q-value is at most the identification level (0.01 by default).
