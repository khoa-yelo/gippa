Detection enrichment asks whether a function category (GO, KEGG, COG) holds more or fewer detected transcription units (TUs) than expected. A TU is detected when any of its genes is, and its detection probability comes from the detectability model. The p-value is exact under the law of detected TUs in the category given the run's total, with Benjamini-Hochberg q per collection and direction; for GO over-representation, TUs of significant child terms are removed from their parents first. The effect is detected minus expected TUs. These p-values are not calibrated and are not discovery claims. The category summary compares detected and expected genes, with a TU-block bootstrap interval.
