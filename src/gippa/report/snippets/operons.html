For neighbouring genes in one predicted operon that are both detected, the log2 abundance difference is fitted with a Student-t whose spread grows at low abundance. A pair far from the typical within-operon difference is called discordant at a Benjamini-Hochberg q of at most 0.1 by default. Missed operons are same-strand neighbours not predicted to share an operon whose abundances are close. Their FDR uses convergent and sandwiched neighbour pairs, which cannot share a transcript, as decoys, reweighted to the intergenic distances of the candidates.
