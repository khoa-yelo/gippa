The input carries decoy peptides, and enough of them (80 % by default) pair with a gene. For each gene, the best accepted target peptide competes with the best decoy paired to it: the better one wins, and a tie goes to the decoy. A decoy not mapped to one gene competes alone, as a decoy win. The gene q-value is the picked FDR: decoy wins plus one, divided by target wins. A gene is detected when its q-value is at most the identification level (0.01 by default). The plus one sets a floor: no q-value is below 1 over the number of genes won by a target, so a run with few such genes may detect none.
