The input carries decoy peptides, and enough of them (80 % by default) pair with a gene. For each gene, the best accepted target peptide competes with the best paired decoy: the better one wins, and a tie goes to the decoy. The gene q-value is the picked FDR, decoy wins scaled up by the share of decoys that paired, plus one, over target wins. A gene is detected when its q-value is at most the identification level (0.01 by default). The plus one sets a floor: no q-value is below 1 over the number of genes won by a target, so a run with few such genes may detect none.
