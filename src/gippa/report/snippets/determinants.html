The determinants model asks how much of the variation in log2 abundance the gene's features explain, in blocks: transcription, translation, codon use, protein and genomic context. An elastic net and LightGBM are fitted with cross-validation over transcription units. The elastic net's drop in R² when a block is removed is the primary attribution; SHAP values describe the LightGBM fit only. Permuting whole transcription units gives a p-value for the model R², which is uncalibrated and not a discovery claim. Genes whose abundance lies more than two SD from the prediction are flagged. The model needs at least 100 genes with measured abundance in at least 25 transcription units; otherwise none is fitted.
