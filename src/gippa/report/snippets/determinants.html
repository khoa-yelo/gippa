The determinants model asks how much of the variation in log2 abundance the gene's features explain, in blocks: transcription, translation, codon use, protein and genomic context. An elastic net and LightGBM are fitted with cross-validation over transcription units. The elastic net's drop in R² when a block is removed is the primary attribution; SHAP values describe the LightGBM fit only. Permuting whole transcription units gives a p-value for the model R², which is uncalibrated and not a discovery claim. Genes whose abundance lies more than two SD from the prediction are flagged. With fewer than 100 genes, no model is fitted.
