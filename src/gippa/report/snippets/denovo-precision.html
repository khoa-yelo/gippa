De novo peptides are binned by score decile and split into length classes. In each, the share matching the genome's six-frame translation exactly is compared with the share matching a shuffled decoy and with the share of confidently identified reference peptides of that class matching the genome (0.99 when none are given). A length class is left out of a bin when its reference match rate is less than 0.05 above the decoy match rate; a bin with no class left has no estimate. Otherwise the estimate is the fraction of distinct de novo peptides in the bin that are correct, with a bootstrap interval. One minus its lower bound is each peptide's prior error in the placement FDR.
