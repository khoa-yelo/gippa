De novo peptides are binned by score decile. In each bin, the share that matches the genome's six-frame translation exactly is compared with the share that matches a shuffled decoy, and with the share of confidently identified reference peptides that match the genome (assumed to be 0.99 when no reference peptides are given). From these shares, the estimate is the fraction of distinct de novo peptides in the bin that are correct, with a bootstrap interval. One minus its lower bound is each peptide's prior chance of being wrong in the placement FDR. A bin whose target and decoy match rates are too close is not reported.
