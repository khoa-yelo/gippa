`gippa report --redact` writes a shareable report: the provenance command line shows `<input>/<file name>` for absolute paths, `<outdir>` for the output directory and `<profile>` for the profile. The Gene detection section shows the detection summary as two views (genes detected by q threshold, and observed over expected detection by group) instead of its first rows. When the fully inlined report would reach 20 MB, the novel ORF locus views are linked to their SVG files under `plots/` instead of being inlined.
