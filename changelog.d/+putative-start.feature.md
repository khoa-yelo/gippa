Variant sites that look like translation starting at an unannotated alternative start codon (a GTG, TTG or other configured non-ATG start read as methionine by peptides that all begin at that residue, and, inside a gene, not directly after K or R) now carry the new flag `putative_start` in `variants.tsv` and the VCF, and their confidence is capped at `candidate`; counts, calls and class error rates are unchanged. The variants figure now writes the residue J as "I/L", shows the top 15 sites with "(top 15 of N; all in tables/variants.tsv)" in its title when there are more, and states "class error unestimated" once instead of on every line.
