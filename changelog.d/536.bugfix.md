`gippa run --proteins` without `--peptides` (with or without `--abundance`) now produces the gene table and the core analyses through the protein path: miniprot, then tblastn, the mapping files, `tables/genes.tsv`, the context, operon and determinant tables, and the report. Before, such a run produced only the annotation and the report. The detection tier is `protein_list` and the gene q-value is not estimated. Variants and novel ORFs need peptides and are skipped. Protein records of 7-40 aa go to the peptide path only with `--peptides`; without it they are not used, and the new preflight warning `proteins.short_records_unused` gives their count (a `--strict` run exits 3).
