`gippa run --proteins` without `--peptides` (with or without `--abundance`) now produces the gene table and the core analyses through the protein path: miniprot, then tblastn, the mapping files, `tables/genes.tsv`, the context, operon and determinant tables, and the report. Before, such a run produced only the annotation and the report. The detection tier is `protein_list` and the gene q-value is not estimated. Variants and novel ORFs need peptides and are skipped. Protein records of 40 aa or fewer now reach the peptide path also without `--peptides`.
